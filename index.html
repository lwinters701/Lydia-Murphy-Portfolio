meet the designer

Hi, I'm Lydia.

I'm a UX/UI designer and front-end developer with five years of agency experience across a wide range of industries and brand voices. Starting out as a developer changed how I design — I understand how interfaces actually get built, so what I design is beautiful, realistic, and easy to hand off.

My process lives at the intersection of creativity and clarity, from user flows to high-fidelity prototypes in Figma. I've also leaned into AI-assisted design and development tools like v0 and Claude Code, which let me move from concept to working product faster and communicate ideas more clearly along the way. If a problem involves people, screens, and a blank canvas, I'm in my element.

Outside of work, I'm usually outside — hiking, tending to my small flock of chickens, or just spending time around animals in general.
